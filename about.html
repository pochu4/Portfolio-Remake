About Me

Hello again — I'm Johann, a Marketing & Brand Designer based in Vancouver, working across brand identity, digital marketing, and web design, with a background in UX/UI and front-end development.

My Path So Far

Right now, I'm working as a Marketing & Brand Designer, where I lead brand identity, web, and digital marketing work across a mix of projects. From brand systems and website design to campaign and content coordination. I'm also exploring opportunities in marketing and brand design roles, and staying open to UX/UI and product design, where my research and interaction design background still comes into play.

I studied New Media Design & Web Development at BCIT, where I learned to connect design and code through hands-on projects that combined UX strategy, UI design, and front-end development. Before that, I completed the Marketing Management – Communications program, which built my foundation in storytelling, brand positioning, and understanding audiences. All of these experiences now shape how I approach design.

Recently, I've also done some work as a Design & Web Development Intern at Convergence / Pixel Ramen, a design and dev agency based in Vancouver. I contributed to branding, visual content, and web builds, while also supporting QA and front-end development for live client projects.
